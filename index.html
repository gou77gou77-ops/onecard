3. 광고와 동의

앱은 Google AdMob을 통해 광고를 제공합니다. Google은 광고 제공과 측정을 위해 광고 식별자 등 일부 정보를 수집할 수 있습니다.

  • 유럽경제지역(EEA)·영국·스위스 이용자: 앱을 처음 실행할 때 Google의 동의 관리 도구를 통해 맞춤 광고와 데이터 사용에 대한 동의 여부를 선택할 수 있습니다. 동의하지 않아도 앱은 이용할 수 있으며, 이 경우 맞춤형이 아닌 광고가 표시될 수 있습니다. 선택은 앱의 설정 > "광고 동의 설정 변경"에서 언제든 바꿀 수 있습니다.
  • 모든 이용자: Android 기기의 설정 > 개인정보 보호(또는 Google) > 광고에서 광고 ID를 재설정하거나 삭제하여 맞춤 광고를 제한할 수 있습니다.

Google의 데이터 처리 방식: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

One Card Online Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026 · Effective: October 19, 2026

susuim ("we", "the developer") respects the privacy of everyone who uses One Card Online ("the App"). We comply with applicable laws, including the Korean Personal Information Protection Act and the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information We Collect

The App has no sign-up. We never ask for your name, phone number, email address or government ID.

During online matches, your nickname, profile icon and title are visible to the other players in the same room.

2. How We Use It

  • To run online matches with friends (creating and joining rooms, playing, checking connection status)
  • To save your game progress
  • To check whether an app update is required
  • To show ads and grant rewards for watching rewarded ads
  • To find errors and improve stability
  • To respond to your inquiries

Legal bases (GDPR): performance of the service you request (online play, saving progress), our legitimate interests (security, stability, non-personalized ads), and your consent (personalized ads).

3. Ads and Consent

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect information such as your advertising ID to serve and measure ads.

  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: When you first open the App, Google's consent tool asks whether you agree to personalized ads and related data use. You can use the App without agreeing; you may then see non-personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > "Ad consent settings".
  • All users: You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Privacy or Google > Ads) to limit personalized ads.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Service Providers and International Transfers

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed only by the providers below to run the App.

5. Retention and Deletion

  • Data on your device (progress, settings, nickname): deleted immediately when you uninstall the App or clear its data.
  • Online room data: kept only while needed for the match and deleted within 30 days after it ends.
  • Anonymous UID: no longer used once you uninstall the App, and deleted promptly on request.
  • Advertising data: handled under Google's retention policies.

6. Your Rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and withdraw your ad consent at any time. If you are in the EEA or UK, you also have the right to data portability, the right to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Send requests to the email below. Because the App has no accounts, including your nickname and roughly when you played helps us find your data. We respond within one month.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country, up to 16 in the EEA). If we learn that we have collected personal information from such a child without parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

8. Security

  • All server communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).
  • We do not collect directly identifying information and use only an anonymous ID.
  • Access to server data is kept to a minimum.
